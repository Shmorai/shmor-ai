What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a similar way. We call them all "cookies" here.

Cookies on Shmor.ai

NameTypePurposeDuration
shmor-consentStrictly necessary (local storage)Remembers your cookie choice so we don't ask againUntil cleared
_ga, _ga_*Analytics (optional)Google Analytics: counts visits and shows how pages are used. Only set if you click "Accept".Up to 13 months

If we haven't enabled analytics, you won't see a cookie banner and no optional cookies are set. Your region choice (US, UK or EU) is also kept in your browser's local storage (shmor-region) so we can show the right currency and offers. It is never sent to us.

UK and EEA visitors: optional cookies are only set after you click "Accept". US visitors: you can opt out at any time with the button below, and we honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

Cookies set by our partners

When you click a link from Shmor to a retailer or provider, the affiliate network (for example Awin, CJ or Impact) and the provider may set their own cookies on their websites. These record that you were referred by Shmor so the referral can be credited. They're controlled by those companies under their own cookie policies, and you can manage them using the provider's cookie settings or your browser.
