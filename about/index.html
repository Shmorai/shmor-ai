About us

We do the searching, so you don’t have to.

Shmor is a network of specialist marketplaces that bring the best options from trusted brands into one place.

Search more. Do less.

Finding the right vacation, contractor or internet deal shouldn't take an evening of tab-hopping. Most of us visit five or six sites, compare in our heads, and still wonder if we missed something better.

Shmor (short for search more) does that legwork for you. We bring options from established brands into one clear place, line them up side by side, and let you choose on your terms.

One brand, many specialist marketplaces

Rather than one giant site that does everything badly, Shmor is a network of focused marketplaces. Each one is built around a single need, such as travel, local services, deals, internet or energy, with the filters and comparisons that make sense for it.

Every marketplace lives at its own address (like travel.shmor.ai or pros.shmor.ai), and you can reach all of them from one search bar on shmor.ai. We serve shoppers in the United States, the United Kingdom and Europe, with offers and prices matched to where you are.

What we stand for

  • Clarity over clutter. Show what matters and skip what doesn't.
  • Honesty about money. Shmor is free to use because brands may pay us a commission. We explain exactly how that works on our How we make money page.
  • Your choice, always. You decide what to buy and who to buy it from. We just make it quicker to get there.
